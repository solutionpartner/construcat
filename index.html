How a house gets built

Four stages, one contract

01 — SITE

Land and survey

We walk the plot, check access, soil and sun, and tell you honestly what it will cost to build there.

02 — DESIGN

Drawings you own

Plans, sections and a material palette, revised with you until the layout is right. No template floor plans.

03 — BUILD

Our own crews

Timber frame raised in weeks, envelope sealed and tested, trades scheduled against a fixed-price contract.

04 — KEYS

Handover and warranty

Blower-door results, an as-built set of drawings and a ten-year structural warranty in your hands.
